# Speed leveling

The fastest route from level 1 to hero that I know of: where to level at each stage and what gear to pick up on the way. Item links open the item in the [item search](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items).

## Leveling route

| Level | Area | What to do |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Survival Training](../world/areas.md#survival-training) | Immediately get 2 [silver bracelets](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3772&area=survivalTraining) (for leveling only) |
| 1 | Survival Training | Get [girth](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3780&area=survivalTraining), [legguards](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3768&area=survivalTraining), [clawed gloves](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3709&area=survivalTraining) |
| 2 | Survival Training | Get [dented breastplate](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3764&area=survivalTraining), 2 [iron bracers](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3753&area=survivalTraining), 2 [threadbare cloaks](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3705&area=survivalTraining) |
| 3 | Survival Training | Get the [secret rings](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3763&area=survivalTraining) and [floating quartz](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3770&area=survivalTraining) |
| 4 | [Lunatic's Alley](../world/areas.md#lunatics-alley) | Grab the [Asteri hold](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=25388&area=lunaticsAlley), maybe the [shroud](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=25382&area=lunaticsAlley) helmet if you're strong |
| 4-5 | [T'leisins Pool](../world/areas.md#tleisins-pool) | Rip through the gypsy camp to get the [legs](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=12216&area=tleisinsPool) and [feet](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=12217&area=tleisinsPool) from the hunters in the pool |
| 6 | [Deltherian](../world/areas.md#deltherian) | Grab the [warbanner](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=1993&area=deltherian) from the commander |
| 7-9 | [The Hand of Markov](../world/areas.md#hand-of-markov) | Stick to animals and trash mobs |
| 10 | [Hagarth](../world/areas.md#hagarth) | Buy a [jar of glow-moss light](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=6636&area=hagarth) |
| 10 | [Grungel](../world/areas.md#grungel) | Grab [the hide of a river-troll](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=2802&area=grungel) |
| 10 | [Shrine of Barbades](../world/areas.md#shrine-of-barbades) | Kill the guards and wear the [helmet](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=6233&area=shrineOfBarbades) and [chestplate](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=6208&area=shrineOfBarbades) |
| 10 | [Burrowholme](../world/areas.md#burrowholme) | Kill the guard and wear the [shield](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=20730&area=burrowholme) and [bracers](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=20731&area=burrowholme) |
| 10 | [Jasporin](../world/areas.md#jasporin) | Kill a couple of kobolds to get 2x [robes](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=11408&area=jasporin) |
| 11 | [Naerlan](../world/areas.md#naerlan) | Kill 2 guards and wear their [copper rings](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=3364&area=naerlan) |
| 11-12 | The Hand of Markov | Dwarves start at 400 xp; avoid the guards at first |
| 12 | Burrowholme | Buy 2x [cloaks](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=20717&area=burrowholme) |

| 15 | [Haythor Dungeon](../world/areas.md#haythor-dungeon) | Grab 2x [ream bone bracers](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=18923&area=haythorDungeon) and a [skull belt](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=18944&area=haythorDungeon) |
| 15-21 | The Hand of Markov | Dwarf patrols and guards, still 100-200 xp each |
| 21-23 | [The Avian Tree](../world/areas.md#avian-tree) | Mostly go for the avian guards and sentries (`search up`) |

| 23 | [Mines of Noal'xyn](../world/areas.md#mines-of-noalxyn) | Pick up either [a dark helmet](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=10050&area=minesOfNoalxyn) or [a spirit helmet](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=10044&area=minesOfNoalxyn), [lead boots](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=10066&area=minesOfNoalxyn) and [an iron tower shield](https://nathanielinman.github.io/AdventuresUnlimited/#/items?vnum=10076&area=minesOfNoalxyn) |
