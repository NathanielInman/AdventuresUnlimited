## Temple Upgrade Costs
The max stats a mark can upgrade to are:
  - **HP**: level * 4 + rank * 10
  - **MP**: level * 4 + rank * 10
  - **MV**: level * 10
  - **Hitroll**: level / 2 + rank# * 3
  - **Damroll**: level / 3 + rank# * 2
