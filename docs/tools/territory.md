# Territory

Type `territory clan` in the game and it lists the areas your clan holds, but not the ones it doesn't, which is the list you want when you're looking for something to conquer. Paste what it prints here and you get the other list: every conquerable area your clan doesn't hold, with its level range and a link to its directions.

<TerritoryCalculator />

The area list comes from the game's area files: 120 areas have a clan flag to take. Level ranges match [areas](../world/areas.md), where ALL means any level and HERO means level 100 and up. `territory clan` only shows your own clan's areas, so "not yours" covers both unclaimed areas and ones another clan holds. To tell them apart, stand in the area and type `explored`: if your direction sense works, it names the clan in control. A leader's list adds the room each flag stands in and who planted it, and the table shows both.
