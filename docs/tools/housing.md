# Housing

Price a house or manor before you take your diamonds to the staff. Pick what you want and the total adds up as you go, item by item. Every setting lands in the address bar, so copying the link sends someone your exact build.

<HousingCalculator />

Prices come from the game's own help files (`help house`, `help upgrade_house`, `help manor_house`, `help manor_tools` and `help henchmen`). Upgrades are paid in diamonds; the gold figure is just diamonds at 110 gold each, for comparing. The rank you need for a manor, and how much each rank costs, is on [entitlement and housing](../world/housing.md).

A few things the calculator leaves out because they're one-off services rather than upgrades: redecorating (20 diamonds a room or object), rekeying a lock (250, key not included), a replacement key (50) and removing a permanent guest (20). The lot itself isn't in the total either: a middle lot with one exit is 500 diamonds and a corner lot with two is 750, unless you win one at auction.
